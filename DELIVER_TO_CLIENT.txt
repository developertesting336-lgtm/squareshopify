================================================================================
WHAT THE CLIENT NEEDS TO DO
================================================================================

BEFORE PAYMENTS START:

1. Create Shopify Custom App
   - Go to Shopify Admin
   - Settings → Apps and integrations → Develop apps
   - Create new app
   - Copy credentials
   - Give to you

2. Provide Square Credentials
   - Go to developer.squareup.com
   - Get Access Token, App ID, App Secret
   - Give to you

3. Remove CartDNA
   - Go to Shopify Admin
   - Apps and integrations
   - Find CartDNA
   - Click Remove/Uninstall
   - Confirm

4. Test First Payment
   - Add product to cart
   - Go to checkout
   - Select "Square" payment
   - Use test card (you'll provide)
   - Verify payment appears in Square dashboard

AFTER PAYMENTS START:

1. Check Orders
   - Shopify Admin → Orders
   - See all customer orders
   - Process refunds if needed

2. Check Payments
   - Square Dashboard → Transactions
   - See all payments received
   - Track money flow
   - Monitor success rate

3. Wait for Settlement
   - Money appears in Square account immediately
   - Settles to bank account in 1-3 days
   - Check regularly

4. No Monthly Tasks
   - System runs automatically
   - No maintenance needed
   - No ongoing fees

COMMON QUESTIONS FROM CLIENTS
================================================================================

Q: Do I need CartDNA anymore?
A: No! Remove it completely. This system replaces it.

Q: Are there monthly fees?
A: Only Square's standard processing fees (typically 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction).
   No monthly subscriptions.

Q: When do I get the money?
A: Usually within 1-3 business days after payment is processed.

Q: What if a customer wants a refund?
A: You can process refunds in your Square dashboard.
   It's easy and takes a few clicks.

Q: Is it secure?
A: Yes! All payments are encrypted and processed by Square (PCI Compliant).

Q: What if something breaks?
A: Contact [your name/email]. I'll help troubleshoot and fix it.

Q: Can I modify it later?
A: Yes! If you need changes, just let me know and we can update it.

Q: Is there a setup fee?
A: One-time fee: $250 (already paid)

Q: Any other costs?
A: Hosting costs vary but typically $0-50/month.
   Only if you need paid tier (free options available).
